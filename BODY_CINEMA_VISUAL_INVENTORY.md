# Body Cinema: Visual Proof Inventory

This document defines the exact visual assets required to make Body Cinema a demonstration-first flagship experience. Every claim must be proven with motion before the creator is asked to upload a video.

## 1. The "Magic" Intake Showcase (Pre-Upload)

The top of the page cannot be a static upload box. It must be a living demonstration of the transformation.

**Required Assets:**
*   **Source vs. Cinematic (Side-by-Side):** A looping video showing a raw, unedited phone clip on the left, and the finished, color-graded, cinematic Body Cinema output on the right.
*   **The Intelligence Overlay:** A looping animation showing the system "thinking" over a video—tracking motion, framing the face, and analyzing lighting. This proves "We understand your motion."
*   **The Final Drop:** A looping preview of what the creator actually sells (e.g., a high-end PPV teaser or a Telegram drop).

## 2. Treatment Previews (The Vibe)

Every treatment card must be a real, looping video. No static icons. No placeholders.

**Required Assets (Looping MP4s or WebMs):**
*   `preview-curves-360.mp4`: A 360-degree orbit shot.
*   `preview-arch.mp4`: Sculpted side light, lower form focus.
*   `preview-waist.mp4`: Editorial push-in on the waistline.
*   `preview-abs.mp4`: Clean definition, high contrast.
*   `preview-thigh.mp4`: Tight composition, slow motion.
*   `preview-silhouette.mp4`: Backlit, graphic outline.
*   `preview-lower-back.mp4`: Low-key light, intimate detail.
*   `preview-hips.mp4`: Rhythmic lateral motion.
*   `preview-legs.mp4`: Vertical framing, runway pacing.
*   `preview-back.mp4`: Noir edge light.
*   `preview-decollete.mp4`: Soft editorial light.
*   `preview-mirror.mp4`: Reflections and depth.

## 3. The "Expert Team" Analysis Animation (Post-Upload)

When the user uploads, they must see the system working.

**Required Asset:**
*   A dynamic, animated sequence (CSS/React) that visually steps through the analysis process over the uploaded video thumbnail:
    *   "Detecting strongest moments..."
    *   "Finding highest-converting hook..."
    *   "Mapping body movement..."
    *   "Building cinematic sequence..."

## Acceptance Criteria

1.  **Zero Empty Space:** Every section contains motion.
2.  **Zero Imagination Required:** If the UI says it does something, there is a video showing it doing that thing.
3.  **Screenshot Test:** Any screenshot of the page looks like a premium, expensive creative product, not a developer form.
