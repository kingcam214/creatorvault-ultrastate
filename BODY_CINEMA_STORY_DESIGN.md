# Body Cinema: Demonstration-First Story Design

## Objective
Transform the `/vault-x/studio` route from a functional form into a cinematic showcase that proves value immediately.

## 1. The Hero Section: "The Dream"
*   **Visual:** The `hero-transformation.mp4` video plays automatically. It dominates the top of the screen.
*   **Message:** "Your Body. Your Empire."
*   **Action:** The upload button sits below this undeniable proof of quality. The creator already wants the result before clicking.

## 2. The Intelligence Overlay: "The Magic"
*   **Trigger:** When a video is uploaded, the static spinner is replaced by `intelligence-overlay.mp4`.
*   **Visual:** The video shows glowing tracking lines and facial recognition grids over a cinematic background.
*   **Message:** "Cinematic Intelligence Active." The checklist (Detecting moments, Mapping movement, Finding hooks) appears alongside the video, making the analysis feel like a high-end service, not a script running.

## 3. The Treatment Grid: "The Menu"
*   **Visual:** The static treatment cards are replaced by 12 looping videos (`preview-arch.mp4`, `preview-silhouette.mp4`, etc.).
*   **Message:** "Set the vibe."
*   **Experience:** The creator doesn't read descriptions; they *see* the editorial styles in motion. Hovering over a video could subtly increase its opacity or brightness, making the grid feel alive.

## 4. The Final Drop: "The Outcome"
*   **Trigger:** When a treatment is selected and the creator proceeds to the final review step.
*   **Visual:** The `final-drop.mp4` teaser trailer plays alongside the "Immutable Production Record."
*   **Message:** "Prepare the drop."
*   **Experience:** The creator sees the kind of high-converting asset they are about to lock in. It connects the technical act of "recording a governed draft" to the business act of "creating a product to sell."

## Layout Rules
*   **No empty black boxes:** If a video isn't loaded yet, a cinematic gradient or subtle motion background must fill the space.
*   **Seamless transitions:** Moving between steps (Upload -> Preset -> Configure) should feel fluid, not like loading a new webpage.
*   **Mobile-first:** The looping videos must scale perfectly on mobile devices, ensuring the "wow" factor isn't lost on smaller screens.
