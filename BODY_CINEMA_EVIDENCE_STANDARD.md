# Body Cinema Evidence Standard

**Status:** Mandatory product law  
**Effective:** 2026-08-06  
**Applies to:** Every Body Cinema capability, treatment, output, quality claim, and competitor comparison

> **No feature report is acceptance evidence.** A Body Cinema capability is incomplete until it can be watched, compared, and traced to a real creator-owned source clip.

## The Acceptance Rule

Code, test results, screenshots, mockups, written descriptions, animation, and labels are never sufficient to declare a Body Cinema feature complete. The creator and founder must be able to watch the claimed behavior operate on source footage and inspect why the system made its recommendation.

A capability may be described only with one of these statuses.

| Status | Meaning | Permitted language |
|---|---|---|
| **Proven** | A complete watchable record exists, including the canonical source clip, visible workflow, output, comparison, and acceptance decision. | “Proven on the Gold Standard Library.” |
| **Demonstrable but unproven** | The user interface or non-spending logic exists, but no real-source result has been captured. | “Ready for proof; not yet proven.” |
| **Incomplete** | The required source handling, result, comparison, or evidence does not exist. | “Incomplete.” |

No release note, sales page, or product screen may describe a feature as intelligent, high-converting, superior, premium, or world-class unless the required evidence record exists.

## Required Proof by Claim

### “Real video previews”

The proof record must show all twelve treatment previews as real files, played on desktop and mobile. The record must demonstrate that each preview loops, is materially different from every other preview, matches the named treatment, and contains neither a placeholder nor a duplicate asset.

### “AI analyzes the upload”

The proof record must include a screen recording of a real creator-owned source video moving through Body Cinema. It must visibly show the strongest hook, strongest thumbnail, strongest scenes, body focus, recommended treatment, and the reasons for every recommendation. A decorative animation is not evidence of source-specific analysis.

### A named treatment, including “The Arch”

The same canonical source clip must be run through at least four materially different treatment plans: **The Arch**, **Silhouette**, **Luxury Reveal**, and **VIP Tease**. The resulting outputs must be viewed side by side. If the outputs are substantially similar in shot plan, pacing, composition, visual treatment, or intended payoff, the treatment system fails acceptance.

### “Body Cinema beats CapCut”

A valid comparison must use the same legal, creator-owned source clip in Body Cinema and in the named comparator. The evidence record must show the inputs, the workflows, final outputs, creator time required, and a blind or independent quality assessment. If CreatorVault is not clearly better in quality or creator workflow, it may not claim superiority.

## Gold Standard Library

The Gold Standard Library is CreatorVault’s permanent regression benchmark. It contains no material without documented creator ownership and consent.

Each entry must retain the following artifacts:

| Artifact | Required record |
|---|---|
| **Canonical source** | Creator ownership and consent record, source version, duration, orientation, and checksum. |
| **Expected treatment plans** | The full timecoded plans for every supported treatment. |
| **Expected outputs** | The accepted reference output for each treatment, versioned and retained. |
| **Decision evidence** | Hook, scene, thumbnail, body-focus, treatment rationale, and creator approval. |
| **Comparison evidence** | Prior accepted output and current candidate placed side by side. |
| **Acceptance record** | Named reviewer, acceptance or rejection reason, and date. |
| **Regression decision** | A required decision when a new change makes any treatment worse or causes treatments to converge. |

Every future change affecting source analysis, treatment planning, rendering, editing, quality review, or packaging must regenerate the applicable Gold Standard Library examples. A release is blocked when a treatment regresses against its accepted canonical output or becomes indistinguishable from another treatment.

## Non-Negotiable Safety and Truth Rules

1. Never use a demo asset as proof of a creator-specific outcome.
2. Never use a creator source clip or output without documented ownership and consent.
3. Never make a paid provider call, spend credits, publish, message, schedule, or attach checkout as part of evidence collection without an explicit, bounded authorization.
4. Never hide a rejected output, degraded treatment, or failed comparison from the evidence ledger.
5. Never use a simulated conversion claim as measured revenue.

## Evidence Ledger Fields

Every Body Cinema claim must map to a ledger entry containing the claim, status, source identifier, output identifier, proof recording, comparison set, decision, reviewer, date, and known limitations. Missing fields make the claim incomplete.
